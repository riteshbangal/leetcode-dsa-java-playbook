# Roadmap

This roadmap lists the planned pattern-first learning order. Only patterns that have been studied and documented are marked complete.

Completing a pattern means being able to:

- Recognize its clues.
- Explain why it applies.
- State its invariant.
- Implement it independently in Java.
- Analyze its complexity.
- Revisit mistakes without memorizing a particular solution.
